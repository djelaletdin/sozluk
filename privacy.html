Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Welcome to Sada Sozluk. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed through our application.

1. Personal Data Collection

Sada Sozluk does not require account registration or user sign-in. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information, such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, location data, or contact lists.

2. Analytics and Crash Reporting

To improve the stability, speed, and overall quality of the application, we use third-party services provided by Google: Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. These services collect anonymized, non-personally identifiable data, which may include:

  • Device details (device model, operating system version).
  • App crash reports and performance diagnostics.
  • General usage statistics (features used, search frequency counts).

This information is used strictly to identify bugs and enhance the app experience. It is never linked to your personal identity.

3. Children's Privacy

Sada Sozluk is safe for children of all ages. We do not knowingly collect or request personal information from children under the age of 13.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated modification date.
