Support

Welcome to the support page for Sada Sozluk. If you have questions, feedback, or need help with the application, you can find common answers below or get in touch with us directly.

Does the application require an internet connection?

No. Sada Sozluk stores the dictionary database locally on your device. You can search for word definitions and access all features without an active network connection.

How can I clear my search history?

On the home screen, find the recent searches section. Tap the clear option located next to the section header to remove all items from your search history.

How do I add words to my favorites?

While viewing the detailed definition page of any word, tap the star icon at the top right of the screen. This saves the word to your favorites list for quick offline retrieval.

What should I do if I find an incorrect definition or face a bug?

Please contact us via the support email address below. We verify database corrections and application issues regularly and include fixes in upcoming updates.
